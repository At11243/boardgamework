## Verification basis

Both assigned appearances of 114 drafts were individually checked against original VASSAL artwork, the imported module appearance traits, and UICFB symbols/size/service references. The other 86 are security fronts with parenthesized strengths and assumed blank reverses; their retrieved physical candidates were checked too.

**Verified concerns digital counter metadata.** With VASSAL as the working reference, an explicit module Flip or Emplace-as-CD trait plus readable artwork can establish the digital pair even without an exact physical photo match. This does not assert that the physical copy was found. Physical findings and unsuccessful searches remain in the audit/history. **An assumed blank still requires physical evidence**; no unconfirmed blanks were promoted.

Confidence greater than 95% is review judgment, not a statistical accuracy guarantee. Editor completeness alone does not establish verification. No gameplay effects or historical organization were inferred from filenames.
