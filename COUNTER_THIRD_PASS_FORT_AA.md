# Targeted third pass: fortified regions and infantry AA — 2026-10-30

Reviewed **55 existing In progress definitions**: **39 fortified-region counters and all five infantry counters became Verified**. **11 remain In progress**: nine unlocated UR pairs and two physical/digital candidate discrepancies. Totals are now **5,027 Verified / 1,941 In progress / 0 Unreviewed**.

This is one targeted third-pass group, not a completed third pass of the entire remaining inventory. No further editor code changes were needed. The known missing layouts were added in the preceding editor-format update; unresolved labels, symbol meanings, missing assets, variants and physical matches are still evidence questions. A future counter may reveal another layout requirement.

## What was established

Fortified-region designations, symbols and both printed levels were compared with the original VASSAL artwork and paired native KIC108 photos, including neighbouring cells. R16a 4.A.5, printed p20 / PDF22 supports the UR level layout. Alternate faces remain **Other**; no reduced-strength interpretation, movement, combat strength or zero/N/A was invented.

The two **15 UR** entries represent distinct observed level pairs: **1/2 (241266)** and **1/4 (241267)**. Their existing type IDs and pair assignments were retained. Rotated close views corrected candidate row mix-ups for **1/44 vs 2/44**, **I/68 vs II/68**, and **I/51 vs II/51** before acceptance.

UICFB PDF p2 identifies the small number inside/beside the unit symbol as an AA rating. The extra **1** on infantry **395, 99, 112, 14 and 52** was recorded independently for each printed face. **52 prints it on both VASSAL faces**, and the enlarged final physical reverse comparison also shows it. The other four reverse AA fields were left blank when no value is printed. Original combat ratings, face statuses, sizes and artwork were preserved. No gameplay effects were implemented.
